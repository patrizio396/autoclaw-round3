## 1. DEFINITIONS

[Page 2]

**1.1 "Background IP"** means Intellectual Property owned or licensed by a Party before the Effective Date, or developed independently of the Services, including Vendor Tools, pre-existing libraries, frameworks, models, and know-how.

**1.2 "Deliverables"** means the work product expressly identified in a Statement of Work as deliverables, excluding Vendor Tools, Background IP, generic methodologies, and residual know-how.

**1.3 "Intellectual Property"** or "**IP**" means all patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, moral rights, database rights, and other intellectual property rights worldwide.

**1.4 "Services"** means the professional services described in a Statement of Work.

**1.5 "Vendor Tools"** means Vendor's proprietary software, SDKs, agents, pipelines, pretrained models, configurations, and development tools, including improvements and derivatives thereof.

**1.6 "Statement of Work"** or "**SOW**" means a statement of work executed by authorized representatives of both Parties referencing this Agreement.

## 2. SERVICES; STATEMENTS OF WORK

**2.1** Vendor shall perform the Services described in each SOW in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted practices in the software-engineering industry.

**2.2** In the event of conflict, the order of precedence is: (a) this Agreement; (b) the applicable SOW; (c) any attached exhibits, except that a SOW may expressly override a specified Section of this Agreement if it identifies the Section by number.

**2.3** Neither Party is obligated to enter into any SOW. Each SOW is a separate engagement; fees under one SOW do not cross-collateralize another.

## 3. ACCEPTANCE

[Page 3]

**3.1** Vendor shall submit each Deliverable for Customer's review. Customer has **ten (10) Business Days** after receipt (the "**Acceptance Period**") to test the Deliverable against the written acceptance criteria in the SOW.

**3.2** If Customer does not provide a written notice of rejection specifying non-conformities in reasonable detail before the end of the Acceptance Period, the Deliverable is **deemed accepted**.

**3.3** If Customer timely rejects, Vendor shall cure the specified non-conformities and resubmit. One resubmission cycle of ten (10) Business Days applies. A Deliverable rejected twice for the same non-conformity may be escalated to the executive sponsors.

**3.4** Use of a Deliverable in production, or any use other than evaluation, constitutes acceptance.

## 4. FEES; PAYMENT; TAXES

**4.1 Fees.** Customer shall pay the fees set forth in the applicable SOW. Unless the SOW states otherwise, fixed-fee Deliverables are invoiced **monthly in advance** based on the payment schedule in the SOW, and time-and-materials Services are invoiced monthly in arrears.

**4.2 Payment Terms.** Undisputed invoices are due **net thirty (30) days** from invoice date. Disputed amounts must be noticed in writing within fifteen (15) days of invoice receipt, stating the basis in reasonable detail; undisputed portions remain payable.

[Page 4]

**4.3 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts past due accrue interest at the lesser of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law. Vendor may suspend Services on ten (10) days' written notice if undisputed amounts remain unpaid.

**4.4 Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of sales, use, VAT, and similar taxes. Customer is responsible for all such taxes other than taxes on Vendor's income.

**4.5 No Set-Off.** Customer may not set off amounts owed under this Agreement against any other claim, except for amounts finally determined to be owing by a court or arbitral tribunal of competent jurisdiction.

**4.6 Expenses.** Pre-approved, reasonable out-of-pocket expenses are reimbursable at cost, with supporting receipts.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

**5.1 Background IP; Vendor Tools.** Each Party retains all right, title, and interest in and to its Background IP and, in the case of Vendor, its Vendor Tools. No rights are granted in Background IP or Vendor Tools except the limited licenses expressly stated in this Agreement or the applicable SOW.

**5.2 Work Product.** Upon payment of the applicable fees, Vendor hereby assigns to Customer all right, title, and interest in and to the Deliverables (excluding Background IP and Vendor Tools). Vendor shall execute documents reasonably requested to perfect such assignment. To the extent any Deliverable is a "work made for hire" under 17 U.S.C. § 101, it shall be deemed a work made for hire commissioned by Customer, with Vendor as author only to the extent required to effect the assignment.

[Page 5]

**5.3 License to Customer.** With respect to Vendor Tools and Background IP embedded in or required to use the Deliverables, Vendor grants Customer a **perpetual, irrevocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable (except to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all assets), royalty-free license** to use, reproduce, and display such items solely as embedded in the Deliverables and solely for Customer's internal business operations. No source-code license, sublicense, or reverse-engineering right is granted except as required by applicable law.

**5.4 Residual Knowledge.** Nothing restricts Vendor from using Residual Knowledge. "**Residual Knowledge**" means ideas, concepts, know-how, and techniques retained in unaided memory, excluding Customer Confidential Information and excluding source code.

**5.5 Open Source.** Vendor may use Open Source Software ("**OSS**") licensed under OSI-approved licenses in the Deliverables, provided Vendor does not incorporate copyleft components (including GPL, AGPL, and MPL, except LGPL dynamically linked) in a manner that would require distribution of Customer-proprietary code. Vendor shall provide an OSS notice listing material OSS components.

**5.6 Feedback.** Customer grants Vendor a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use Feedback to improve Vendor products and services, without obligation to Customer.

**5.7 Reservation.** All rights not expressly granted are reserved.

## 6. CUSTOMER DATA; DATA PROTECTION

[Page 6]

**6.1 Customer Data.** Customer retains all right, title, and interest in Customer Data. Customer grants Vendor a limited license to process Customer Data solely to perform the Services.

**6.2 Security.** Vendor shall maintain administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards no less protective than industry standards for similarly situated vendors, including encryption in transit and at rest for systems processing Customer Data.

**6.3 Breach Notice.** Vendor shall notify Customer of a confirmed Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Data without undue delay and in any event within **seventy-two (72) hours** after confirmation.

**6.4 DPA.** If required by applicable privacy law, the Parties shall execute Vendor's standard data processing addendum.

## 7. CONFIDENTIALIALITY

**7.1** Each Party shall protect the other Party's Confidential Information with at least the care it uses for its own similar information, and no less than reasonable care.

**7.2** Obligations survive for **five (5) years** after termination, and for trade secrets for so long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

**7.3** Compelled disclosure is permitted with prior notice where legally allowed.

**7.4** Residual Knowledge under Section 5.4 is not Confidential Information of Customer.

## 9. INDEMNIFICATION

**9.1 Vendor Indemnity.** Vendor shall defend Customer against third-party claims alleging that a Deliverable, as delivered by Vendor and used by Customer in accordance with this Agreement, infringes a third party's Intellectual Property, and shall indemnify Customer from resulting damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement, **subject to Section 10**.

**9.2 Exclusions.** Vendor has no obligation under Section 9.1 to the extent the claim arises from (a) Customer Data, Customer specifications, or Customer's combination of the Deliverable with items not provided by Vendor; (b) use outside the scope of the license or after Vendor notified Customer of a claim and provided a reasonable workaround; (c) modifications not made by Vendor; or (d) OSS used in accordance with Section 5.5.

[Page 8]

**9.3 Customer Indemnity.** Customer shall defend Vendor against third-party claims arising from (a) Customer Data (including claims that Customer Data infringes IP or violates law); (b) Customer's instructions or specifications; or (c) Customer's use of the Deliverables outside the licensed scope, and shall indemnify Vendor from resulting damages finally awarded or agreed in settlement, subject to Section 10.

**9.4 Procedure.** The indemnified Party shall provide prompt notice, reasonable cooperation, and control of the defense to the indemnifying Party. The indemnified Party may participate with its own counsel at its own expense. No settlement imposing a non-monetary obligation or admission on the indemnified Party requires its consent, not to be unreasonably withheld.

**9.5 Infringement Remedy.** If a Deliverable is held to infringe or Vendor believes it may, Vendor may, at its option and expense: (a) procure the right to continue use; (b) replace or modify it to be non-infringing without material loss of functionality; or (c) if neither is commercially reasonable, terminate the affected Deliverable and refund prepaid unused fees for it. This Section 9.5, together with Section 9.1, states Vendor's entire liability and Customer's exclusive remedy for IP infringement.

## 10. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

**10.1 Exclusion of Consequential Damages.** NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, CONSEQUENTIAL, PUNITIVE, OR EXEMPLARY DAMAGES, OR FOR LOST PROFITS, REVENUE, OR BUSINESS, WHETHER IN CONTRACT, TORT (INCLUDING NEGLIGENCE), STRICT LIABILITY, OR OTHERWISE, EVEN IF ADVISED OF THE POSSIBILITY OF SUCH DAMAGES.

[Page 9]

**10.2 Liability Cap.** EACH PARTY'S AGGREGATE LIABILITY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT SHALL NOT EXCEED THE FEES PAID OR PAYABLE TO VENDOR UNDER THE APPLICABLE SOW IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE FIRST EVENT GIVING RISE TO THE CLAIM (the "**Liability Cap**").

**10.3 Carve-Outs.** The exclusions in Section 10.1 and the cap in Section 10.2 do not apply to: (a) a Party's fraud or willful misconduct; (b) a Party's infringement or misappropriation of the other Party's Intellectual Property; (c) Customer's payment obligations; or (d) Vendor's indemnity obligations under Section 9.1, which are capped at **two times (2×) the Liability Cap**.

**10.4 Allocation of Risk.** The Parties agree the fees reflect the allocation of risk in this Section 10, which is an essential basis of the bargain.

**10.5 Independent Allocations.** The exclusions and caps are independent and survive even if a limited remedy fails of its essential purpose, except where a court of competent jurisdiction finally determines the failure is unconscionable.

## 11. TERM; TERMINATION

**11.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of **twenty-four (24) months**, automatically renewing for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least **sixty (60) days** before the then-current term ends.

**11.2 Termination for Convenience.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience on **ninety (90) days'** prior written notice. Customer shall pay all fees for Services performed and expenses incurred through the effective termination date. Prepaid fees for undelivered fixed-fee Deliverables are non-refundable except as expressly stated in a SOW.

[Page 10]

**11.3 Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate for material breach if the breach remains uncured **thirty (30) days** after written notice specifying the breach in reasonable detail; or immediately if the other Party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases business.

**11.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination: (a) Customer's license to Vendor Tools embedded in accepted Deliverables survives in accordance with Section 5.3; (b) Customer shall pay accrued unpaid fees within thirty (30) days; (c) each Party shall return or delete the other Party's Confidential Information upon request; (d) Sections 5 (as to ownership), 7, 8.4, 9, 10, 12, 14, and 15 survive.

**11.5 Transition.** For thirty (30) days after notice of termination, Vendor shall, at Customer's request and at then-current rates, provide reasonable transition assistance not to exceed the remaining committed scope.

## 14. GOVERNING LAW; DISPUTE RESOLUTION

**14.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Delaware**, excluding its conflict-of-laws rules and the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods.

**14.2 Informal Resolution.** The Parties shall first escalate any dispute to their executive sponsors, who shall confer in good faith for fifteen (15) days.

**14.3 Arbitration.** Any dispute not resolved informally shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, in Wilmington, Delaware, in English, before a single arbitrator. The award is final and may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction. Each Party shall bear its own costs; the arbitrator may allocate AAA fees. **EACH PARTY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A JURY TRIAL AND TO PARTICIPATE IN A CLASS ACTION.**

**14.4 Injunctive Relief.** A Party may seek provisional injunctive relief in any court of competent jurisdiction to protect IP or Confidential Information, without waiving arbitration.

**14.5 Confidentiality of Proceedings.** The arbitration, the award, and all submissions shall be Confidential Information.

## 15. GENERAL

**15.1 Notices.** Notices must be in writing and are deemed given when delivered by hand, one (1) Business Day after deposit with a nationally recognized overnight courier, three (3) Business Days after mailing by certified mail, or on the date of transmission by email with confirmation of receipt, addressed to the addresses on the first page (or a notice address designated in writing).

**15.2 Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed, except that either Party may assign this Agreement to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, corporate reorganization, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, upon written notice.

[Page 12]

**15.3 Entire Agreement; Amendments.** This Agreement, including its SOWs and exhibits, is the entire agreement and supersedes all prior negotiations. Amendments must be in a writing signed by both Parties. Preprinted terms on a purchase order or portal do not amend this Agreement.

**15.4 Severability; Waiver.** If a provision is held unenforceable, it shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable. A waiver must be in writing. No failure to enforce is a waiver.

**15.5 Relationship.** The Parties are independent contractors. This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, or agency.

**15.6 Force Majeure.** Neither Party is liable for delay or failure to perform (other than payment) caused by events beyond its reasonable control, provided the affected Party gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate.

**15.7 Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, including electronic signatures.

## 3. ACCEPTANCE

[Page 3]

**3.1** Vendor shall submit each Deliverable for Customer's review. Customer has **thirty (30) Business Days** after receipt (the "**Acceptance Period**") to test the Deliverable against the written acceptance criteria in the SOW, **and such period shall be extended for any period during which Customer reasonably requires additional testing, or if Vendor fails to provide complete documentation.**

**3.2** Customer's failure to reject a Deliverable is **not** acceptance. Customer may reject a Deliverable at any time during the Acceptance Period **or within thirty (30) Business Days after production use if a previously undiscoverable defect appears.**

**3.3** If Customer timely rejects, Vendor shall cure the specified non-conformities **and all related deficiencies** and resubmit, restarting the Acceptance Period in full. **There is no limit on the number of rejection cycles.** A Deliverable is not accepted until Customer provides written acceptance, which Customer may withhold in its reasonable discretion.

**3.4** Use of a Deliverable in production **does not** constitute acceptance.

## 4. FEES; PAYMENT; TAXES

**4.1 Fees.** Customer shall pay the fees set forth in the applicable SOW. Unless the SOW states otherwise, fixed-fee Deliverables are invoiced **upon Acceptance of the applicable milestone**, and time-and-materials Services are invoiced monthly in arrears **following Acceptance of the associated Deliverables**.

**4.2 Payment Terms.** Undisputed invoices are due **net ninety (90) days** from invoice date. Customer may dispute any invoice in whole or in part at any time before payment, **and may withhold any amount reasonably related to a rejection, dispute, open defect, or pending audit.**

[Page 4]

**4.3 Late Payment.** Undisputed amounts past due accrue interest at the lesser of one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, **commencing only after Vendor has given a second written notice and fifteen (15) additional days have passed.** Vendor may suspend Services on **thirty (30) days'** written notice if undisputed amounts remain unpaid, **provided Customer has not invoked the dispute process, an audit, or a rejection.**

**4.4 Taxes.** Fees are exclusive of sales, use, VAT, and similar taxes. Customer is responsible for all such taxes other than taxes on Vendor's income. **Customer may withhold tax where required by law and shall cooperate in good faith to eliminate or reduce withholding.**

**4.5 Set-Off.** Customer may set off amounts owed under this Agreement against any claim Customer asserts in good faith under this Agreement or any SOW, **including refund, indemnity, warranty, and audit claims, whether or not liquidated.**

**4.6 Expenses.** Pre-approved, reasonable out-of-pocket expenses are reimbursable at cost, with supporting receipts, **subject to the audit rights in Section 4.7.**

**4.7 Audit.** During the term and for **three (3) years** after, Customer or its independent accountant may, upon **ten (10) days'** notice and no more than **twice per year**, audit Vendor's books and records reasonably related to the fees charged under this Agreement, during business hours and without unreasonable disruption. If an overcharge of more than five percent (5%) is found, Vendor shall bear the reasonable cost of the audit.

**4.8 Holdback.** Customer shall be entitled to withhold **twenty percent (20%)** of each fixed-fee invoice as a holdback until **ninety (90) days after Acceptance** of the final Deliverable under the applicable SOW, released only if no open defects remain.

## 11. TERM; TERMINATION

**11.1 Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of **twelve (12) months**, automatically renewing for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either Party gives written notice of non-renewal at least **ninety (90) days** before the then-current term ends.

[Page 10]

**11.2 Termination for Convenience.** **Customer** may terminate this Agreement or any SOW for convenience on **fifteen (15) days'** prior written notice. **Vendor may not terminate for convenience.** Upon such termination Customer shall pay fees for Services **accepted** through the effective termination date, **and Vendor shall refund all prepaid fees, including for work in process, within ten (10) Business Days. Vendor waives any claim for wind-down costs, lost profits, or termination fees.**

**11.3 Termination for Cause.** **Customer** may terminate immediately, without notice or cure, for (a) any material breach by Vendor; (b) any breach of Section 5, 6, or 7, whether or not material; (c) insolvency, bankruptcy, or cessation of business; or (d) **any act or omission that Customer reasonably determines puts Customer's systems, data, or reputation at risk. Vendor may terminate for Customer's material breach only if the breach remains uncured sixty (60) days after written notice, and only as to the affected SOW, not this Agreement.**

**11.4 Effect of Termination.** Upon termination: (a) **all licenses granted to Customer survive in perpetuity, including any license to Vendor Tools, and Customer's ownership of Deliverables is unaffected;** (b) Customer shall pay accrued unpaid fees for accepted Deliverables within **ninety (90) days**; (c) each Party shall return or delete the other Party's Confidential Information upon request, **except Customer may retain copies required by law or for archival, audit, or litigation-hold purposes;** (d) Sections **5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11.4, 12, 14, and 15** survive; (e) **Vendor shall, within five (5) Business Days, deliver to Customer all work in process, source code, credentials, and documentation, including incomplete items;** (f) **Vendor shall not delete, encrypt, or withhold any Customer system or Deliverable as self-help.**

**11.5 Transition.** For **ninety (90) days** after notice of termination, Vendor shall, at Customer's request and **at no charge**, provide reasonable transition assistance, including knowledge transfer, attendance at Customer meetings, and reasonable engineering support, not to exceed **forty (40) hours per week.**
